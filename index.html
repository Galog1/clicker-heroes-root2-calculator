            About and credits

            Based on the Ancient Calculator by graceoflives, which was inspired by Shruikan864's Rules of Thumb 1.0 spreadsheet and sugima's 1.0 Rules of Thumb post. Thanks to Kragnir for the better estimates of the NUA ancients, princeandrew01 for the alpha formula and dfslkwe for the faster search.

            The Ancients calculator uses the rules of the original as of March 2018 (v1.0e10, no level limits). This rebuild keeps them and drops every outside dependency, so it keeps working when other sites go away. Everything runs in your browser and your save never leaves it.

            The Outsider planner is based on driej's Outsider Recommender for the root2 mod of Clicker Heroes, and follows the root2 rules, which differ from the regular game. Ancient souls decide the breakpoint, the outsider levels and the end of run estimates.
